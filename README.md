# ⚽ FIFA World Cup Analysis Dashboard (Tableau)

An interactive Tableau dashboard that explores the FIFA World Cup from **1930 to 2014**: who won, who reached the finals, which countries hosted, and how the tournament has grown over the years.

## 🎯 Objective
To answer questions such as: Which countries have won the most World Cups? Which teams reach the semi-finals and finals most often? Who hosted the tournament? How have goals, matches and qualified teams changed over time?

## 📌 Key Insights
- **Brazil** has the most titles (5), followed by **Italy** (4) and **Germany FR** (3).
- **Uruguay** and **Argentina** have 2 titles each. **Spain, Germany, France and England** have 1 each.
- Mexico, Italy, Germany, France and Brazil have each hosted the tournament more than once.
- **Belgium** has the most World Cup appearances among the teams shown (13), followed by USA, Scotland, Paraguay, Hungary and Germany FR (12 each).
- Goals scored, matches played and qualified teams have all grown steadily from 1930 to 2014.

## 🧩 Dashboard Sections
| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Countries that Won the World Cup | Winner flags with the number of titles |
| Teams by Total Finals and Semi-finals | How often each team reached the semi-finals and finals |
| Countries that Hosted the World Cup | Number of times each country hosted |
| Countries Qualified to the World Cup | Appearances by team |
| Competitions Status by Year | Goals scored, matches played and qualified teams per tournament |
| Winners and Top Teams | Table of winner, runner-up, third, fourth, host country and final stadium by year |
| Maps: Audience by Country and Year | Map of audience by country |
